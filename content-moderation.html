Content Moderation Policy

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

DoCo is committed to maintaining a safe platform for all users. This policy outlines how we moderate content and handle violations.

Reporting Content

Users can report objectionable content through several methods:

  • Post Reporting: Tap the menu icon (•••) on any post and select "Report Post"
  • User Reporting: Visit a user's profile, tap the menu icon, and select "Report User"

All reports are confidential. The reported user will not know who submitted the report.

Blocking Users

You can block any user to prevent them from:

  • Seeing your posts on the map
  • Viewing your profile
  • Following you

To block a user, visit their profile and select "Block User" from the menu. You can manage blocked users in Settings > Privacy & Safety > Blocked Users.

Review Process

  1. Report Received: A report is submitted by a user
  2. Initial Review: Our team reviews the report within 24 hours
  3. Action Taken: Based on the review, appropriate action is taken
  4. Notification: The reporting user is informed of the outcome

Content Categories

Immediate Removal
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Terrorism or violent extremism
  • Direct threats of violence
  • Non-consensual intimate images
Reviewed Within 24 Hours
  • Hate speech and discrimination
  • Harassment and bullying
  • Nudity and sexual content
  • Self-harm and suicide content
  • Graphic violence
  • Spam and scams
Reviewed as Reported
  • Misinformation
  • Impersonation
  • Intellectual property violations
  • Other policy violations

Enforcement Actions

Depending on the severity and frequency of violations, we may take the following actions:

  • Warning: First-time minor violations receive a warning
  • Content Removal: Violating content is removed from the platform
  • Temporary Suspension: Account is temporarily disabled (1–30 days)
  • Permanent Ban: Account is permanently terminated for severe or repeated violations

Users may appeal enforcement actions by contacting our support team.

Appeals

If you believe your content was removed in error, or if your account was suspended unfairly, you can appeal through our Contact Support feature in Settings, or by emailing docoappdirect@gmail.com. We will review appeals within 48 hours.
