Community Guidelines

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

DoCo is a map-based social platform that connects people through shared locations and experiences. These guidelines help ensure a safe and welcoming community for everyone.

❤️ Be Respectful

  • Treat others with kindness and respect
  • Embrace diversity and different perspectives
  • Engage in constructive conversations
  • Respect others' privacy and personal boundaries

✋ No Harassment or Bullying

  • Do not target, intimidate, or threaten others
  • No stalking or unwanted repeated contact
  • Do not share others' personal information without consent
  • Report abusive behavior immediately

🛡️ No Hate Speech

  • No content promoting hatred based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin
  • No symbols or imagery associated with hate groups
  • No dehumanizing language or stereotypes

🚫 No Inappropriate Content

  • No nudity or sexually explicit content
  • No graphic violence or gore
  • No content promoting self-harm or suicide
  • No content depicting or promoting illegal activities

✅ Be Authentic

  • Do not impersonate others or create misleading profiles
  • Share genuine experiences and authentic content
  • Do not spread misinformation or fake news
  • Do not use the platform for spam or commercial solicitation

📍 Respect Location Privacy

  • Do not share exact locations of private residences without permission
  • Be mindful when posting about sensitive locations
  • Do not use location features to track or monitor others

Reporting Violations

If you see content that violates these guidelines, please report it using the report button on any post or user profile. Our team reviews all reports within 24 hours. You can also block users to prevent them from interacting with you. See our Content Moderation Policy for details.

Enforcement

Violations of these guidelines may result in:

  • Content removal
  • Temporary account suspension
  • Permanent account ban

The severity of enforcement depends on the nature and frequency of violations.
